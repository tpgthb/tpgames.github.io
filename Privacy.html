Calc Chain – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 October 2026

Calc Chain is a number puzzle game made by TP-games. This policy explains how the game handles your information.

We collect no data

Calc Chain does not collect, store, share or sell any personal information. There are no user accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Data stored on your device

Your highest score and settings (such as light/dark theme) are saved only on your own device so the game remembers them. This information never leaves your device and is not sent to us or anyone else. You can delete it at any time by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.

Contact

Questions? Email sonderso@yahoo.com.

Privatlivspolitik (dansk)

Calc Chain er et talspil lavet af TP-games. Spillet indsamler, gemmer, deler eller sælger ingen personoplysninger. Der er ingen konti, ingen reklamer, ingen analyse og ingen sporing.

Din højeste score og dine indstillinger gemmes kun på din egen enhed og sendes aldrig videre. Du kan slette dem ved at rydde appens data eller afinstallere appen.
