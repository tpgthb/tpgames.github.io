How to play
  • 0–999 points: Classic Match — match two numbers that are adjacent to each other (including diagonally and across line breaks) which are either equal or add up to 10. Select this mode with the [=] icon.
  • 1,000–2,999 points: Addition [+] unlocks. Select the mode, tap two numbers in order, and they merge into one result number.
  • 3,000–4,999 points: Subtraction [−] unlocks. The first number you tap is the left side of the equation (e.g. 8 and 6 become a 2), and the result may never go negative.
  • 5,000–6,999 points: Multiplication [×] unlocks.
  • 7,000–9,999 points: Division [÷] unlocks. If it doesn't divide evenly, the move is rejected.
  • 10,000+ points: Equation Mode — build a chain of operators, then tap the numbers in order (each must be adjacent to the previous one). If any step along the way produces a non-integer result, the entire move is rejected immediately.
  • The timer is optional — turn it on and pick between 1 and 60 minutes, or play entirely without a countdown.
  • Your records are saved on this device, separately for each time frame. "Highest" is your best score ever.
  • Tap "Share result" to create an image card with your score, level and time frame. In a timed game it unlocks when the time is up.
